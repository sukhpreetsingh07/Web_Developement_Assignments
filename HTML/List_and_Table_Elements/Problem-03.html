SEQUENCE BEFORE ATTENDING PRACTICAL CLASS

  1. Wear your Official College ID Card and Lab Coat before entering the lab.
  2. Submit your Practical Record File to the lab assistant for verification.
  3. Log in to your assigned computer terminal using your Roll Number.
  4. Listen carefully to the pre-lab instruction lecture given by the faculty.
  5. Execute the assigned program and get the output signed by the instructor.


ACADEMIC & CLASSROOM RULES

  • Rule 1: Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory to appear in end-semester exams.
  • Rule 2: Mobile phone usage during lectures is strictly prohibited.
  • Rule 3: Students must maintain silence and discipline inside academic blocks.
  • IMPORTANT ACADEMIC NOTICE: Mid-Semester Examinations will commence from next Monday. Ensure all pending assignments are submitted on time.


ACADEMIC TERMINOLOGY & DEFINITIONS

Lecture
Interactive classroom session conducted by faculty to teach theoretical concepts.

Lab
Hands-on practical session in computer workstations or hardware labs.

Tutorial
Small group problem-solving and doubt-clearing session.

Internal Assessment
Continuous evaluation including mid-terms, quizzes, and class attendance.

Practical
Experimental examination testing technical implementation skills.

ANNUAL COLLEGE FEST 2026 — IMPULSE '26

Welcome to IMPULSE 2026, the largest three-day annual socio-cultural and technical college festival celebrating talent, creativity, innovation, and youth energy!




FEST INTRODUCTION

IMPULSE 2026 brings together students from over 100+ colleges across the country to compete in dance, music, coding, robotics, drama, and fine arts. Join us for 3 unforgettable days filled with electrifying performances, competitions, and pro-nights!

STEP-BY-STEP REGISTRATION PROCESS

REGISTRATION DEADLINE: 10th October 2026 (11:59 PM IST)


  1. Visit the Official Fest Registration Portal using the link provided in the contact section below.
  2. Fill in your basic details (Name, College Roll Number, Email, and Department).
  3. Select the events you wish to participate in from the available category checklist.
  4. Upload a valid copy of your College Student ID Card for participant verification.
  5. Pay the nominal registration fee via UPI / Net Banking and save your payment receipt.
  6. Receive your unique Fest Registration Pass Code via registered email.


GENERAL FEST RULES & CODE OF CONDUCT

  • Mandatory Requirement: Carrying a valid College Physical ID Card is compulsory for campus entry.
  • Prohibited Items: Possession of alcohol, tobacco, drugs, or hazardous materials is strictly forbidden.
  • Discipline Code: Any misbehavior or damage to college property will lead to immediate disqualification and disciplinary action.
  • Reporting Time: Participants must report at the event venue at least 30 minutes prior to the scheduled time.
  • IMPORTANT NOTICE: Registration Pass Code must be shown at the main entrance gate for security clearance.


FEST TERMINOLOGY & DEFINITIONS

Open Event
An event open to students from all colleges without any preliminary qualification test.

Team Event
A competition requiring a group of 2 to 10 participants representing a single institution.

Individual Event
A solo competition designed for individual participant performance.

Registration
The formal process of signing up and paying the fee for event participation.

Coordinator
A student or faculty lead responsible for managing a specific event or venue.

Final Round
The ultimate stage of a competition where top qualifiers compete for prize awards.

Official University Admission Handbook 2026-27

Welcome to the official admission portal. Here you will find comprehensive guidance regarding our academic programs, application procedures, eligibility criteria, required documents, and fee structures.




ADMISSION OVERVIEW

Our university offers world-class undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs designed to foster research, innovation, and career excellence. We invite aspiring students to apply for the upcoming academic session.



STEP-BY-STEP APPLICATION PROCESS

APPLICATION DEADLINE: 31st July 2026 (11:59 PM IST)


  1. Register on the Official University Admission Portal by creating a student account.
  2. Fill out the personal, academic, and contact details accurately in the online application form.
  3. Upload scanned copies of all mandatory documents in the prescribed format.
  4. Pay the non-refundable application fee online through Credit Card, Debit Card, or Net Banking.
  5. Submit the completed application form and download the confirmation receipt for future reference.
  6. Appear for the entrance examination or merit evaluation as per the scheduled dates.


ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

  • Undergraduate Programs: Minimum 60% aggregate marks in 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board.
  • Postgraduate Programs: Minimum 55% aggregate marks in relevant Bachelor's degree from an accredited university.
  • Mandatory Requirement: Valid score card in qualifying national/university level entrance test.


REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR VERIFICATION

  • Class 10th Marksheet & Passing Certificate (Proof of Date of Birth)
  • Class 12th Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • Graduation Marksheets & Degree Certificate (For PG Applicants)
  • Category / Caste Certificate (If applicable)
  • Migration Certificate & Transfer Certificate (Original)
  • Recent Passport-size Photographs (4 Copies)
  • Valid Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID)


IMPORTANT ADMISSION TERMS & DEFINITIONS

Eligibility
The minimum academic qualifications and criteria required to apply for a program.

Merit List
Ranked list of applicants selected based on entrance test scores and qualifying exam marks.

Counselling
Interactive seat allocation session where candidates choose their preferred branch and seat.

Scholarship
Financial aid awarded to meritorious or economically weaker students to support tuition fees.

Registration
Formal enrollment of the admitted candidate into the university database after fee payment.

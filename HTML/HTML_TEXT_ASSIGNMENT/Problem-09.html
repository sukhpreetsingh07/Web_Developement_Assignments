Student Code of Conduct & Rules

General Rules

All students must carry their official university identity cards at all times while present on campus premises.
Failure to produce valid identification upon request may lead to restricted access.

Attendance

Maintaining an overall attendance record of at least 75% in every course is mandatory for all students.
Exemptions will only be granted for approved medical emergencies submitted with appropriate documentation.

Examination

Possession of unauthorized electronic gadgets during examinations is strictly prohibited. Any student caught breaking exam integrity rules will face immediate disciplinary action.

Assignment Submission

Assignments must be submitted on or before the declared due date.
Late submissions will attract a deduction penalty of marks.

Discipline

Respectful conduct towards faculty, staff, and fellow peers is expected from every member of the academic community.

Note: These rules are enforced to ensure an inclusive, fair, and productive learning environment for everyone.

About University

GLA University is a premier educational institution committed to academic excellence, innovative research, and student success. Established in 2010 , our university has grown to serve over 600-700 students across diverse academic disciplines.

Departments

Our academic structure includes the Department of Computer Science,Department of Business,Department of Electronic and Communication engineering . Each department focuses on providing high-quality theoretical and practical knowledge.

Academic Programs

We offer undergraduate, graduate and masters programs. Students gain hands-on knowledge in various domains, preparing them for leadership roles in competitive global industries.

Campus Facilities

The campus features modern research laboratories, a central library with physical and digital resources, sports complexes, and spacious auditorium facilities. Specialized science labs measure experimental spaces in m2 and fluid capacities in m3.

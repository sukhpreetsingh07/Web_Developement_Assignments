Science Reference Notes

Chemistry

Water molecular formula: H2O

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen Gas: O2

Sulfuric Acid formula: H2 SO4

Glucose formula: C6 H12 O6

Physics

Unit of Area: m2 (square meters)

Unit of Volume: m3 (cubic meters)

Mass-Energy Equivalence: E = mc2

Einstein's theory of relativity revolutionized modern physics.

Biology

Photosynthesis uses light energy to convert CO2 and H2O into glucose and oxygen.

Quick Revision

Key Concept: Chemical subscript numbers like in H2O indicate the number of atoms, whereas exponents like m2represent units of measurement.

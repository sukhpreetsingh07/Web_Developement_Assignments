About GLA University

GLA University is a premier private university located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh (the land of Lord Krishna). Spanning over 110 acres, the campus provides world-class education in Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Computer Applications, and Applied Sciences.

"Our mission is to impart value-based education and generate new knowledge by engaging in cutting-edge research and innovation."

--- Office of the Chancellor, GLA University

Admissions & Announcements

- Admissions Open for Academic Year 2026-2027

GLA University invites applications for undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs. Candidates with an aggregate of 50% in qualifying examinations are eligible to apply for GLAET (GLA Entrance Test). Early bird scholarships are available for high achievers euro & dollar grants applicable for international exchanges.

Important Date: Last date for early registration is 31st May 2026.

  • Graduation Degree (for PG applicants)

Admission Procedure (Step-by-Step)

  1. Fill out the Online Application Form below.
  2. Appear for GLAET (GLA Online Entrance Test) or submit national test scores (JEE / CAT / CUET).
  3. Attend the Online / Offline Counseling Session.
  4. Submit required documents for verification.
  5. Pay the initial course fee to secure admission.

Admissions Glossary & Guidelines

Eligibility
Minimum marks requirement in qualifying exam (typically > 50% aggregate).
Scholarship
Financial aid offered to meritorious candidates scoring > 80% in GLAET or national qualifying exams.
Merit List
Ranked list of applicants based on entrance test performance and academic history.
Application Fee
A non-refundable registration fee of Rs.1,000 (INR) payable during form submission.

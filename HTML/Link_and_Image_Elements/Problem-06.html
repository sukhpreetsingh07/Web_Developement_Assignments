About The Event

Welcome to the biggest annual technological extravaganza of the year! TechFest 2026 is organised by the College Cultural and Technical Committee to bring together bright young minds, tech enthusiasts, developers, and designers under one platform. Over three action-packed days, students from across the country will compete, collaborate, and showcase groundbreaking ideas.

Whether you are passionate about building autonomous robotics, deploying full-stack web applications, cracking cybersecurity challenges, or designing futuristic UI/UX interfaces, TechFest 2026 offers something special for every innovator.

Event Highlights

Prepare yourself for intense competition, hands-on learning, and networking opportunities with industry professionals.

  • 24-Hour Hackathon: Build creative software or hardware solutions to real-world industrial challenges.
  • Robotics Arena: Test your engineering skills in RoboWars, Line-Follower competitions, and Drone Races.
  • Coding Arena: Speed coding and algorithmic bug-hunting challenges designed to test your logic.
  • Tech Expo: Showcase your college semester projects and startup prototypes to potential investors and mentors.

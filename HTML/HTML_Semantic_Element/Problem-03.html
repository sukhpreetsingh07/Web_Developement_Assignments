Appointment Booking Steps

  1. Fill in the appointment request form with complete patient details below.
  2. Select the required medical department and preferred specialist.
  3. Choose a convenient consultation date and time slot.
  4. Receive confirmation via email and SMS with an appointment registration token.
  5. Report to the AIIMS Bathinda OPD registration counter 30 minutes prior to your slot.

Key Healthcare Definitions

Consultation:
A formal meeting with a specialist doctor for diagnostic evaluation, medical advice, and treatment planning.
Follow-up:
A subsequent review visit to evaluate treatment progress and adjust medications if required.
OPD:
Outpatient Department — the clinical section where medical care and consultations are provided without hospital admission.
Appointment:
A scheduled time slot designated for a patient to consult with a specific medical officer.
Emergency / Casualty:
24/7 acute care unit equipped for immediate treatment of critical illness or severe traumatic injuries.
